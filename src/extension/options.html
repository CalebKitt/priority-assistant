Your privacy
With AI on, the text of the emails it reads is sent to Google's Gemini. If you add your own paid Gemini key below, your emails stay private: Google doesn't use them for training. With the team's key (or a free key), Google may use them to improve and train its AI.

Your own key (optional). Create one at Google AI Studio and turn on billing for it to be paid. It is saved in your own Google Drive/OneDrive app folder so your phone gets it too.

On your phone

The phone app has the same priority inbox, swipe to sort, and a hands-free mode that reads your priority email aloud and takes replies by voice. Sign in there with the same account and it picks up everything learned here.

Setup check

Privacy: your email goes only to Google/Microsoft (your mail provider) and, only if you turn on AI features, to Gemini for summaries and drafts. What the assistant learns is stored in your browser and in a hidden app folder in your own cloud storage. Nothing is kept on our servers.
