Install Priority Assistant

Thanks for testing! It takes about 3 minutes. You'll need Google Chrome on a computer, and a Gmail or Outlook account.

On your computer

  1. Unzip it and put the folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents. Chrome runs the extension from that folder, so don't delete it. Windows: right-click the zip → Extract All. Mac: double-click the zip.
  2. Open Chrome's extensions page. Copy this into Chrome's address bar: chrome://extensions Chrome doesn't let websites link there directly.
  3. Turn on "Developer mode" with the switch in the top right corner.
  4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the folder you unzipped (the one with manifest.json in it).
  5. Connect your email. The settings page opens on its own. Click Connect Gmail or Connect Outlook.
    Google will warn you, and that's expected. You'll see "Google hasn't verified this app". Click Advanced, then Go to Priority Assistant. On the next screen, tick every box. Without the Gmail and Drive permissions, it can't sort your email.
    Work or school Outlook accounts may say "Need admin approval". That means your IT department blocks apps like this. Use a personal account instead.
  6. Pin it (optional): click the puzzle piece next to Chrome's address bar, then the pin next to Priority Assistant.
  7. Open Gmail or Outlook. It studies your last 60 days of email first, which takes about a minute. Then your inbox shows up sorted by priority. Use the Priority view switch to flip back to your normal inbox.

On your phone

  1. On your phone, open this site's phone app and sign in with the same account. It picks up what it learned on your computer.
  2. Add it to your home screen. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: ⋮ → Install app.
  3. Try the tabs: Inbox, Sort (swipe right for priority, left for trash, up to forward, down for later), Speak (hands-free) and Summary.

Good to know

  • It learns from you. At first it only watches. Once its guesses match yours 85% of the time, it starts making suggestions. If something's in the wrong place, drag it to the right section and it learns from that.
  • It never sends email on its own. It may file email into your existing folders once it's confident. Every move can be undone and shows up under "Moved for you". You can turn this off in Settings.
  • It adds color labels ("Priority: Urgent" and "Priority: Important") so priority also shows in your normal mail apps. You can turn this off in Settings too.
  • AI features are off until you turn them on in Settings. They add summaries, next steps, reply drafts, the daily brief, chat and voice-reply cleanup. With them on, the text of the emails it reads goes to Google's Gemini. If you use your own paid Gemini key, Google doesn't use your emails for training. With the team's key or a free key, Google may use them to improve and train its AI.
  • Chrome may show a "developer mode extensions" warning when it starts. That's because the extension isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet. Keep the extension on.

Getting updates

When there's a new version, download it again from this page, unzip it into the same folder (replace the files), then click the ↻ reload button on Priority Assistant in chrome://extensions and refresh Gmail or Outlook. What it learned is kept.

Removing it

Click Remove on Priority Assistant in chrome://extensions. To also remove its access and saved data, follow the steps in the privacy policy.
