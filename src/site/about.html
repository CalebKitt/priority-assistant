Priority Assistant

Priority Assistant is a student project (Group 11) that sorts your Gmail or Outlook inbox by what matters to you, not just by date.

It watches how you handle email: what you answer first, what you forward and to whom, what you ignore, and which folders you file things into. Then it ranks new email the same way. It can read your inbox aloud, and if you turn on the optional AI features, it also suggests next steps, drafts replies, and summarizes long threads.

Two parts

  • Chrome extension for Gmail and Outlook on the web. It puts a priority list over your inbox, with a sidebar for top priorities and next steps.
  • Phone app at this site. Swipe to sort (right: priority, left: trash, up: forward, down: later), plus a hands-free mode.

What it will and won't do

  • It never sends an email unless you press send.
  • It only starts filing email into folders on its own after it matches your own choices at least 85% of the time, and every move can be undone.
  • What it learns is saved in your own Google Drive or OneDrive app folder, not on our servers.

Right now it is available to invited testers only.
